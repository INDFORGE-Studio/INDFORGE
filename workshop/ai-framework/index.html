01Overview

INDFORGE - AI Framework adds configurable hostile helicopter patrols, military garrisons and ground patrols to compatible Conflict scenarios.

Everything is set from a JSON file in the server profile, without touching the code. Map support depends on the points of interest the scenario exposes.

02Capabilities

  • Combat Heli Spawner. A hostile USSR patrol helicopter appears near the biggest group of players, anchored on a point of interest (military base, airport, city, village…), with a full crew and a configurable AI skill, from Noob to Expert.
  • Scripted flight controller, not the engine's AI flight: patrol loop over nearby points of interest, altitude hold, banked turns.
  • Automatic cleanup. Shot down, pilot or rotor lost, landed or in water: the wreck despawns and the slot is released after a cooldown (23 minutes in total with the shipped values).
  • Military garrisons. Each eligible point of interest gets two patrols, one tight and one wide, and small static teams hold towers, bunkers and watchtowers.
  • Priority under the AI cap. The engine keeps about 128 AI active at once: helicopter crew first, then close patrols and building posts, then wide patrols.

03Setup

  1. Subscribe to the mod and add it to your server's mods.
  2. In the World Editor, add the INDFORGE components to the GameModeCampaign (or base GameMode) entity of your mission.
  3. Copy the shipped ServerProfile/INDF_CombatHeliSpawner/config.json to $profile:INDF_CombatHeliSpawner/config.json. That copy is the one read, once, at startup.
  4. Per world: on the FIA CampaignFactionManager, add USSR to Friendly Factions Ids, and FIA on the USSR one. Without it the two factions fight each other.
  5. Restart the server after every change. In Workbench, close and reopen it: it caches the file.

04Server administration

  • Do not raise MaxActiveHelicopters: the flight is scripted and has no collision avoidance between aircraft.
  • At startup the log must show [INDF_CombatHeliConfig] LoadFromFile SUCCESS, then the POI scan and the garrison population.
  • No helicopter? Check the player count (MinimumPlayersToActivate) and the 23-minute wait after a kill.
  • $profile:INDF_CombatHeliSpawner/config.json
