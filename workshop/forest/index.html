01Overview

INDFORGE - Forest brings the INDFORGE - Conflict PvE experience to the Belleau Wood map: the same AI, doctrine, arsenal, ranks and settings as on Everon, with its own bases, relay stations, roadblocks, patrols and vehicle spawn points.

It requires INDFORGE - Conflict PvE. A change made in Conflict PvE reaches Forest once Conflict PvE is republished on the Workshop.

02Capabilities

  • Conflict bases, relay stations and starting points adapted to Belleau Wood.
  • 15 forest roadblock setups with their guards.
  • Every capturable base defended by 3 to 4 groups: rifle squad, machine-gun team, fire team, AT team.
  • Spetsnaz Overwatch snipers, teams and lone snipers, tracking prey in open fields around the bases.
  • Foot patrols between bases, armed and transport vehicle patrols, civilian presence.
  • AI spawn points named after what they really spawn, for easier admin and editing.
  • Random weapon caches: switched off on this map for now, coming soon.
