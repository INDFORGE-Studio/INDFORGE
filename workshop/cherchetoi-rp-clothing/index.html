01Overview

ChercheToi RP - Clothing is a specific configuration of several clothing mods, with a few changes, for the CHERCHE TOI – RP LIFE role-play community.

02Capabilities

  • Civilian, biker and press outfits gathered from the clothing mods in the dependencies.
  • SAMU emergency-medical equipment, by CTRP team contributor Captain Valaaks.
