01Overview

The UAZ-452 Classic is an off-road van built at the Ulyanovsk plant since 1965. We reworked its design to make a real Bukhanka ready to take on the mountains: 110 hp and more to take you beyond the horizon.

On standby: the mod may be merged with Off-Road Vehicles.

02Capabilities

  • Civilian 4×4 in eight colours: khaki, red, yellow, green, blue, dark blue, purple, pink.
  • Repair version for the Civilian, FIA and USSR factions.
