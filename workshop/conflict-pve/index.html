01Overview

INDFORGE - Conflict PvE turns the Conflict mode into a cooperative PvE campaign on Everon. Players fight as the US; the USSR and the FIA are run by AI with a combat doctrine, a headquarters that acts on intelligence, mortars, drones and helicopters.

Built on Gramps303's ConflictPVERemixedVanilla2.0 and on the WCS, RHS and ACE suites: over 100 Workshop mods, all declared dependencies, nothing repacked. For intermediate to advanced players who enjoy tactical team play. Lone wolves might shed a little tear.

02Capabilities

  • Enemy headquarters. Hunter groups only come on intelligence: an enemy drone or a patrol that spots you or your vehicle. They head where you should be by now, search the area, march in column and spread into a line on contact.
  • Generator capture (with Enhanced Compositions). Repair the FIA HQ generator with a wrench, then fill it with fuel (18 L, about 20 s) while the base siren sounds. Radio contact with your HQ is required.
  • Fuel economy. A running generator gives its base 10 supplies per litre, never more than the base storage holds. A friendly fuel depot or the deployable M1091 fuel truck keeps it full.
  • Sabotage and breaching. About 30 s after a capture, the nearest enemy soldier tries to disable the generator. Wall it in with HESCO and a breacher blows the closest wall: kill him or defuse the charges within 20 s.
  • Hard HESCO. Bullets, grenades and vehicles do nothing. Explosives only: 1, 2 or 4 M112 charges depending on size.
  • AI doctrine. Fire discipline, tactical withdrawal, engagement ranges based on posture, movement, weather and night.
  • Enemy mortars with forward observers, real ballistics and counter-battery play.
  • Overwatch sniper teams: a Spetsnaz sniper with a spotter, or alone.
  • Drone warfare. Enemy drones you can hide from (posture, foliage), jammers, drones kept in saved loadouts.
  • Hunter squads and helicopter paratroopers sized by the number of nearby players.
  • AI rocket launchers also target infantry, in every faction: stay spread out.
  • Modern US AI in MultiCam; enemies with matching camouflage and red armbands; Spetsnaz paratroopers in black and A-TACS.
  • C-RAM that engages all threats, with an attack siren (Main Base only).
  • Ranks saved across server and mission restarts.
  • Complete US arsenal, unlocked at every rank, except the Golden Shovel (Sergeant).
  • The shovel cuts trees, not just bushes, and stumps are removed.
  • Ally markers: icons above allies (50 m, 300 m through optics), squad members in team colour, names up close.
  • Consistent garage and helipad costs: a better vehicle never costs less in supplies, XP or rank.
  • Patrol trucks run either a supply run or a troop transport.

02BIncoming — next update

Merged in development after version 2.0.6, not yet in the Workshop release.

  • Weather director: drizzle, rain, thunderstorm fronts, dawn mist and fog banks with smooth transitions. Bad weather grounds enemy drones and aircraft.
  • Night gear: some enemy soldiers carry night-vision goggles or thermal scopes, and see accordingly.
  • Attack plans: L-shaped attack, hammer and anvil, mortar first, infiltration, feint, harassment and road ambush, chosen from what the HQ knows.
  • Road ambushes: nine kinds, from mines and AT teams to FPV corridors and pre-registered mortar strikes; persistent roadblocks on the roads you use most.
  • Air strikes and Geran raids: guided glide bombs, cruise missiles on captured bases, Su-57 reconnaissance passes, Geran launch trucks you can hunt down.
  • Drone teams: the enemy Mavic no longer drops grenades; it circles above its target, guides strikes, and a light under it shows its state.
  • Radio alerts: short radio hints when a drone watches you, a group comes for you, or a strike is on its way.
  • FIA and regular USSR soldiers in Czech Vz95 uniforms.

04Server administration

  • Our systems are set from $profile:INDFORGE_*/config.json files: AI doctrine, enemy HQ, mortars, generator capture, hunter size, drone sight, name tags, patrol trucks, shovel, session report, and more. The admin console lists every key with its help text.
  • Third-party systems keep their own files: $profile:CET/CETConfig.json (hunters), $profile:MBS_AutoHeliSpawner/config.json (helicopters), $profile:EnhancedCompositions/Garrison.json (garrisons, to switch on).
  • The server runs the version published on the Workshop: an update arrives only after it is republished.

04BAdmin commands

Typed in the in-game chat by a player with the server administrator or session administrator role, or sent through RCON with admin permission. Answers come back in the chat.

#setrank <player name> <rank>Sets a player's rank. Exact in-game name, then the rank name (PRIVATE, CORPORAL, SERGEANT, LIEUTENANT, CAPTAIN, MAJOR…) or its number. The rank is saved across restarts.In service

#aidoctrine mod <name> off		Multiplies every engagement range by a factor, for some seconds or until switched off, to react to an event without a restart.In service

06Latest changelog

Changelog text quoted from the Workshop page, in its original language.

v2.0.6 // 2026-11-18

ENEMY HQ (new) - Enemy hunter groups are no longer sent straight at your exact position. The enemy HQ only reacts to intel: an enemy drone or a patrol that spots you or your vehicle. - It sends a group to where you were last seen, or to where you should be by now: it estimates your direction and speed. - No intel: it searches the area, then the group returns. - Groups march in column and spread into a line on contact. BASE CAPTURE - The generator gives 10 supplies per liter (doubled) and never delivers more than the base storage holds. - If players wall the generator in with HESCO, an enemy breacher now walks to the wall closest to the generator, lays demolition charges and runs clear before the blast. Kill him, or disarm the charges within 20 s. - HESCO are now destructible, but very hard to break: no damage from bullets, grenades or vehicles. Explosives are needed: 1 M112 for the small HESCO, 2 for the long one, 4 for the long and tall one. - The saboteur finds a free side when a vehicle blocks the generator. VEHICLES - Garage and helipad costs reworked: a better vehicle never costs less, in supplies, XP or rank. - The M777 artillery ammo truck is removed: its driving data is broken in game version 1.9. M777 shells stay in the arsenal. ARSENAL / AI - Spetsnaz paratroopers (black and A-TACS) and Mi-8 patrol passengers. - More variety in hunter anti-tank weapons and Overwatch weapons. - Groin plates on the Shaw ARC vests, plus 5-plate AVS vests. MISC - Medical litter is cleared after 120 s. - Enhanced Compositions garrisons spawn even with enemies 50 m away (instead of 200 m). - Server log session report (INDFORGE session lines). - FPVUR Base and FPVUR Ground Robotics are removed from the server mods.
