01Overview

As part of a programme to modernise its infantry, the Freedom and Independence Army (FIA) receives new equipment.

02Capabilities

  • Promet bullpup assault rifle in 6.5×39 mm, 30-round magazine.
  • Two NIJ IIIA ballistic helmets.
  • NIJ IV body armour.
  • Czech Army uniform by _OV_.
