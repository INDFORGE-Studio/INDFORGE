Bigfoot - Radar/Jammer In servicev2.1.0

A “Bigfoot” truck from the large garage turned into an electronic warfare vehicle: it deploys a radar or a jammer, and runs on a diesel generator.

In Conflict PvE 2.1.0

  • Radar: once deployed, it marks moving enemies on the map.
  • Jammer: once deployed, it cuts enemy drones.
  • Cable link with the FP-1 drone launcher truck: the radar's targets show live in the FP-1 mission planner.
  • Enemy direction finding: a radar or jammer that emits too long gets located, then struck (mortar, glide bomb, Geran, sapper raid or FPV).
  • Radio alerts: with an AN/PRC-152 radio, your HQ also warns you of radar contacts and enemy direction finding.
  • M923A1 trucks: stronger engine, gearbox and locking differentials on every US variant, about 70 to 76 km/h, better off road.
  • Server settings: new file $profile:INDFORGE_RadarTruck/config.json, see the Admins page.

Still planned for this truck

  • Emission sounds.
  • Preview image of the truck.

Inspiration: modern AESA radars that double as radar and jammer (sector of about 120°, jamming costs power and heat).

No more server freezes In servicev2.1.0

The freezes that made vehicles stutter and drift when many AI were around are fixed. Patrols, base garrisons and counter-attacks now spawn at a shorter distance, never too close to a player, spread out in time, within a budget of AI soldiers around each player, larger for a group. Far counter-attacks sleep when the whole map is full and wake up when you come close: an enemy recapture is delayed, never blocked. Settings: $profile:INDFORGE_AIBudget/config.json.

Ukrainian front weather In servicev2.1.0

21 episodes by day and by night (clear, still night, mist, low stratus, drizzle, showers, rain, grey front, dry thunder, thunderstorm, night storm, fog banks…), with smooth transitions. Harmless lightning, thunder that arrives at the speed of sound, a full moon every night, a Weather tab for the Game Master. The enemy adapts: drones grounded in strong wind, heavy rain or fog.

00Summary

The enemy no longer knows where you are by magic. Its headquarters acts on reports: what its drones and patrols actually see. It predicts where you are going, chooses a plan, calls its mortars and adapts to weather and night. Stay unseen and it stays blind.

01Command structure

  • Sources. An enemy drone contact, or an enemy group that identifies a player or a player vehicle. Close reports merge into a track: last known position, time, source, on foot or in a vehicle.
  • Dispatch. A fresh track gets a group, spawned on a road far from every player. Drone intel brings special forces; a patrol report brings hunters. A spotted vehicle brings an anti-vehicle team.
  • Prediction. Two reports give your direction and speed. The group heads where you should be by now, not where you were seen.
  • Search. A cold track is searched around its estimated position, on a radius that grows with time, then the group returns.
  • Formations. Column on the march, wedge on approach, line on assault and on contact.
  • Size. Hunter groups and helicopter paratroopers grow with the number of players nearby.

02Combat doctrine

Every enemy soldier follows a doctrine level that sets when he opens fire on infantry, from how far, and how he withdraws.

LevelWhoOpens fire on infantry within (standing / crouched / prone)Withdrawals
BootFIA soldiers, hunters sent on a patrol report275 / 165 / 90 m0

  • Fire discipline. A group that spots you before you see it holds its fire and closes in.
  • Tactical withdrawal. Caught off guard, half the group falls back firing while the other half sprints to cover, then they swap, regroup and come back.
  • Ranges depend on your posture and movement, the weapon, the group size, night and weather. A target that fires loses the benefit of its posture.

03Fire support

  • Mortar batteries set up in an enemy base 900 m to 3.5 km from the target, out of towns, with a crew, sentries and a patrol.
  • An observer is required: a group leader or a recon drone must see you, and you must stay put long enough. Ranging rounds come first, then fire for effect.
  • Counter-battery play: the batteries can be found and destroyed.

04Drones

Enemy drones see you by distance, posture and cover:

Your postureSeen within%
Standing300 m
Crouched180 m
Prone100 m
  • Standing still cuts these ranges by 30 %. Night and weather cut them further.
  • Any foliage between you and the drone hides you — even the bush you hide in. Under 15 m, the drone's own test applies.
  • A drone that loses you hunts where it last saw you for about two minutes.
  • Back-pack jammers stop FPV drones within 50 m.

05Mavic light code

A small light under the enemy Mavic tells you what it is doing. Never on an FPV.

  • Patrolling, nothing spotted
  • Tracking a target
  • Fast red blink: guiding a glide bomb
  • Orange blink: searching where it lost you

06Attack plans

For each track, the HQ scores what it knows — on foot or in a vehicle, still or moving, grouped players, inside a base, night, fog, rain, drones available, mortars, recent losses — then picks a plan:

  1. L-shaped attack — one group fixes you at 200 m, a second flanks at 90°, both assault together.
  2. Hammer and anvil — one group blocks the road ahead of a moving target, the other pushes.
  3. Mortar first — the battery strikes, the group waits out of the danger zone, then assaults.
  4. Infiltration — special forces creep in close, at night or in fog.
  5. Feint — one group shows itself and attacks, the second waits on another axis.
  6. Harassment — a sniper team on high ground plus the mortar, against a strong position.
  7. Road ambush — against a moving vehicle (below).

Escalation. Every group you wipe out nearby makes the next ones bigger and sharper, and favours the mortar.

07Road ambushes

  • The HQ follows the road ahead of your vehicle and sets a kill zone 400 to 600 m further, on the high side.
  • Nine variants: lone AT team, L-shaped, mines and fire, the trap (road closed behind you), the bait (a wreck on the road), FPV corridor, close night ambush, long-range hunter on a crest, and a pre-registered mortar strike at a junction.
  • Persistent roadblocks appear on the paved roads you use most, and move every couple of days.
  • Nobody fires before you enter the zone: the first rocket, then everyone.

08Air and long-range strikes

  • Glide bomb on players on foot that a Mavic keeps in sight — its red light blinks fast. Shoot the drone or break its view.
  • Cruise missile on a base you just took from the enemy. The base siren sounds before impact.
  • Su-57 reconnaissance passes can report you to the HQ.
  • Geran raids from launch trucks parked on roads kilometres away. Kill the truck or its driver and the raid stops.
  • Never on a faction's main base, never danger-close to enemy soldiers, never when the weather grounds aircraft.

09Weather

  • 21 credible episodes, by day and by night, in sequence: clear, still night, night mist, low stratus, hot haze, fair, breezy, clearing, cloudy, overcast, dawn mist, mist, grey front, drizzle, showers, rain, dry thunder, thunderstorm, night storm, storm tail, fog banks — with smooth transitions.
  • Drones are grounded from 10 m/s of wind, heavy rain or thick fog; helicopter drops and waves from 14 m/s; paratroopers from 8 m/s.
  • Fog and rain shorten what mortar observers can see. Thermal sights see through light fog.

10Night

  • Some soldiers get night-vision goggles — more often snipers and special forces, less often by day.
  • What a soldier sees comes from what he carries: naked eye, night vision or thermal scope. Some enemy drones carry a thermal camera.

11Radio alerts

Short radio-style hints reach the players concerned: drone overhead, interception group on the way, mortar, Geran, missile, jet, ambush, friendly fighters chasing the jet, radar contact on the jet, the enemy triangulating your radar or jammer, emitter located. Never the enemy's exact position.

12Base capture

  • Standing in the zone no longer takes a generator base: repair the generator with a wrench, then fill it with 18 L of fuel while the siren sounds. Radio contact with your HQ is required.
  • About 30 s after you take it, the nearest enemy soldier walks to the generator to sabotage it (8 s). Kill him first.
  • Wall the generator in with HESCO and a breacher lays demolition charges on the closest wall: 20 s fuse, kill him or defuse.

13Field survival guide

  1. Stay spread out: enemy rocket launchers also fire at infantry.
  2. Break the line of sight with foliage, go prone, keep still under a drone.
  3. Once spotted, change direction: they aim where you should be, not where you were.
  4. Watch the Mavic light. Red: you are watched. Fast red blink: move, now.
  5. A siren at your base means a strike is coming: get out of the base.
  6. Kill the saboteur before he reaches the generator.
