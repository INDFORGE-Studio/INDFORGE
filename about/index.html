03Doctrine

  • On the shoulders of giants. We build on other creators' mods as declared dependencies and credit them on every page. Nothing repacked.
  • Documented settings. Every server setting ships with its own help text, right next to it in the file.
  • Tested before release. Changes go through a real dedicated-server test bench and a review before they reach the Workshop.
  • Readable by players. The enemy cheats less and tells you more: signal lights on drones that change colour when they spot you, radio hints, sirens before a strike.
  • Respect the licences. Our mods are released under the Arma Public Licence (APL or APL-ND, as stated on each page).
