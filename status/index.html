02In the pipeline

Merged into INDFORGE - Conflict PvE after version 2.0.6, waiting for the next Workshop update:

  • Weather director: drizzle, rain, thunderstorm fronts, dawn mist and fog banks with smooth transitions. Bad weather grounds enemy drones and aircraft.
  • Night gear: some enemy soldiers carry night-vision goggles or thermal scopes, and see accordingly.
  • Attack plans: L-shaped attack, hammer and anvil, mortar first, infiltration, feint, harassment and road ambush, chosen from what the HQ knows.
  • Road ambushes: nine kinds, from mines and AT teams to FPV corridors and pre-registered mortar strikes; persistent roadblocks on the roads you use most.
  • Air strikes and Geran raids: guided glide bombs, cruise missiles on captured bases, Su-57 reconnaissance passes, Geran launch trucks you can hunt down.
  • Drone teams: the enemy Mavic no longer drops grenades; it circles above its target, guides strikes, and a light under it shows its state.
  • Radio alerts: short radio hints when a drone watches you, a group comes for you, or a strike is on its way.
  • FIA and regular USSR soldiers in Czech Vz95 uniforms.
