01Overview

AUC - Vehicles is a specific configuration of several vehicle mods, with a few additional changes, built for the [AUC] Arma Ukraine Community.

02Capabilities

  • BLUFOR: 65 vehicles, unlocked with rank.
  • OPFOR: 55 vehicles, unlocked with rank.
  • INDFOR: 21 vehicles in RHS camouflage.
  • Civilian: 13 vehicles.
