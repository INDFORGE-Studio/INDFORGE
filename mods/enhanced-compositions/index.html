01Overview

Enhanced Compositions gives mission makers, Game Masters and players simpler Conflict compositions, easy to adapt to missions or maps where the Conflict mod is hard to set up, plus a new way to capture bases and AI garrisons that protect buildings.

02Capabilities

  • Compositions: small antenna, ammo storage (WIP), field hospital (WIP), vehicle maintenance and living area for US and USSR, the living area with the full list of AI groups in its menu; small FIA headquarters, tent and hut, each with or without a generator.
  • FIA HQ generator. Starts out of order and dry, cannot be destroyed. Repair it with a wrench (8 s, any faction), fill it with a jerry can: it runs until the tank is empty. A crate in each generator HQ holds a wrench and a jerry can.
  • Commander actions grouped on the HQ table, next to the construction panel: logistics, combat support and construction.
  • Garrisons. Barracks: 5 soldiers patrolling plus the full unit list. Armory, vehicle workshops and fuel depot: a two-person team. Antenna: a two-person recon team.
  • Garrison rules. The first group is free. A wiped group returns after 10 minutes if supplies allow and no enemy is near. Not recruitable, not saved. Corporals and above switch it with the Garrison action.
  • Vehicle service while a guard lives: the fuel depot refuels allied vehicles for free; the workshops repair and rearm them for supplies.
  • Golden Shovel in the US and USSR arsenals, from the rank of Sergeant.
  • With INDFORGE - Conflict PvE only: capture by fuel, supplies from the generator and enemy sabotage.

04Server administration

  • All garrison settings live in $profile:EnhancedCompositions/Garrison.json.
  • The file is created on first launch with "Enabled": false: set it to true to turn garrisons on. The mod never overwrites it.

v1.1.0 // 2026-11-16

V1.1.0 FIA HEADQUARTERS WITH GENERATOR - The original FIA headquarters (tent and hut) has been restored to its version without a generator for missions that do not use the new capture. - New items: ‘FIA Headquarters, Tent (Generator)’ and ‘FIA Headquarters, Hut (Generator)’, with the generator out of order. - The repair key and jerry can are now stored in a crate in every HQ equipped with a generator (the same crate found in small FIA garages), rather than being left on the floor. - All the commander’s actions are grouped together on the HQ table, next to the construction panel: logistics, combat support and construction. Only the commander of the faction controlling the base can see them. - The generator repair and refuelling interactions are now easier to access. - Fix: the ‘Become Commander’ panel was appearing twice in the FIA tent HQ.
