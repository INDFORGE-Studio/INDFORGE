01Overview

Realistic Combat Drones - WCS adds the full set of SalamiBoiDev's drone equipment to the WCS arsenals of the US (BLUFOR) and the USSR (OPFOR), with costs and ranks, plus a drone jammer you can build in Conflict bases.

02Capabilities

  • FPV-RPG drone (125), FPV drone (100), dropper drone (75), recon drone (50), batteries (2), controller and goggles (0): Corporal.
  • Jammer bag (100): Private. Long-range drone antenna (200): Sergeant. Drone command station (300): Lieutenant.
  • TPN-19 radar jammer, buildable in Conflict bases by US, USSR or FIA (cost 300): jams drones within 250 m as soon as it is built. ON/OFF switch on the side door handle, usable by anyone. Generator sounds follow its state. Also available to Game Masters.
  • Friend or foe: a jammer worn, carried or built into a base no longer cuts the drones of its own side and allies. A jammer left on the ground jams everyone.
  • Drone controller in the secondary weapon slot; a mask can be worn with drone goggles.
