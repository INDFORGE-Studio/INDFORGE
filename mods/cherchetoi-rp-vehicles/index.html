01Overview

ChercheToi RP - Vehicles is a specific configuration of 32 vehicle and equipment mods, with a few changes, for the CHERCHE TOI – RP LIFE role-play community.

02Capabilities

  • Cars, SUVs, vans, a bus, pickups, motorbikes, bicycles, karts and boats from the community's favourite vehicle mods.
  • Trucks such as the KamAZ 5350, and the SA342 Gazelle helicopter.
