01Overview

A simple change to the vanilla unflip action that came with Arma Reforger 1.2.0.92: now, even alone, you can push or pull any car or truck, and even the BTR, like a real superhero.

Compatible with all mods. Caution: an overdose of Push-Pull may cause health problems.

02Capabilities

  • Solo push and pull on cars, trucks and armoured vehicles.
  • No dependency, compatible with all mods.

05Dependencies

Ready to paste. The list holds the mod and everything it needs, its prerequisites included, generated from the project file and the Workshop pages. The game itself (Arma Reforger base data) is left out. The server also downloads required dependencies on its own.

No dependency: this mod only needs the base game.
