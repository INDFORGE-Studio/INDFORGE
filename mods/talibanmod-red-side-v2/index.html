01Overview

TalibanMod Red Side V2, made for Task Force Baguette, turns the FIA and the USSR into OPFOR fighters from various rebellions of the Arab world. PvE Conflict available.

02Capabilities

  • New characters, vests and headgear.
  • Tan vehicles.
  • Arab-style civilians and civilian vehicles.
  • Arabic inscriptions on FIA and USSR signs.
