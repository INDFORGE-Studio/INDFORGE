INDFORGE - Conflict PvEv2.0.6 · 2026-11-18
ENEMY HQ (new) - Enemy hunter groups are no longer sent straight at your exact position. The enemy HQ only reacts to intel: an enemy drone or a patrol that spots you or your vehicle. - It sends a group to where you were last seen, or to where you should be by now: it estimates your direction and speed. - No intel: it searches the area, then the group returns. - Groups march in column and spread into a line on contact. BASE CAPTURE - The generator gives 10 supplies per liter (doubled) and never delivers more than the base storage holds. - If players wall the generator in with HESCO, an enemy breacher now walks to the wall closest to the generator, lays demolition charges and runs clear before the blast. Kill him, or disarm the charges within 20 s. - HESCO are now destructible, but very hard to break: no damage from bullets, grenades or vehicles. Explosives are needed: 1 M112 for the small HESCO, 2 for the long one, 4 for the long and tall one. - The saboteur finds a free side when a vehicle blocks the generator. VEHICLES - Garage and helipad costs reworked: a better vehicle never costs less, in supplies, XP or rank. - The M777 artillery ammo truck is removed: its driving data is broken in game version 1.9. M777 shells stay in the arsenal. ARSENAL / AI - Spetsnaz paratroopers (black and A-TACS) and Mi-8 patrol passengers. - More variety in hunter anti-tank weapons and Overwatch weapons. - Groin plates on the Shaw ARC vests, plus 5-plate AVS vests. MISC - Medical litter is cleared after 120 s. - Enhanced Compositions garrisons spawn even with enemies 50 m away (instead of 200 m). - Server log session report (INDFORGE session lines). - FPVUR Base and FPVUR Ground Robotics are removed from the server mods. Enhanced Compositionsv1.1.0 · 2026-11-16
V1.1.0 FIA HEADQUARTERS WITH GENERATOR - The original FIA headquarters (tent and hut) has been restored to its version without a generator for missions that do not use the new capture. - New items: ‘FIA Headquarters, Tent (Generator)’ and ‘FIA Headquarters, Hut (Generator)’, with the generator out of order. - The repair key and jerry can are now stored in a crate in every HQ equipped with a generator (the same crate found in small FIA garages), rather than being left on the floor. - All the commander’s actions are grouped together on the HQ table, next to the construction panel: logistics, combat support and construction. Only the commander of the faction controlling the base can see them. - The generator repair and refuelling interactions are now easier to access. - Fix: the ‘Become Commander’ panel was appearing twice in the FIA tent HQ.
INDFORGE - Forestv0.0.2 · 2026-11-15
- Denser enemy presence: every capturable base now has 3 to 4 defending groups (rifle squad, machine gun team, fire team, AT team). - Fewer two-man sentries: most of them are now full fire teams. - More Spetsnaz Overwatch snipers around the bases. - 10 new foot patrols moving between bases. - Defensive positions reworked: guards now hold their posts properly. - All AI spawn points renamed after what they really spawn, for easier admin and editing.
